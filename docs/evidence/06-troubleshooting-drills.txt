Diagnosis: on a brand-new PVC Postgres takes ~30-40 s to initialise; the backend's retry
budget (DB_RETRIES=15 x 2 s) ran out, it exited on purpose, kubelet restarted it, 2nd start OK.

Lesson (see `helm history`): revision 2 FAILED with a server-side-apply conflict: field .data.DB_HOST is owned by
manager "kubectl-patch", and Helm refuses to overwrite another manager's field. Hand-edited drift blocks upgrades.
Correct fix: undo the drift (kubectl patch back, or apply --force-conflicts) and change config only through Helm values.
